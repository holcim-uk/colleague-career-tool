Your career conversation

A career conversation is time with your manager to talk about how you would like to grow. It is your conversation as much as theirs. Ten minutes here beforehand will help you get the most from it. Everything you write stays in this browser, and nobody sees it unless you send it.

Is there anything that would make development easier for you?

For example flexible timing, learning closer to home or online, support with confidence, or fitting things around caring commitments. Lots of people have something like this, and saying it early helps your manager find options that work. You decide whether to share it.

Share an agenda (optional)

Sending your manager a short note beforehand gives them time to think. It includes what you want from the conversation and your questions, plus anything else you choose to add.

3
During the conversation

Talk

Your manager will listen and ask questions, and you can steer the conversation.

Start with what you want

Say it early, even if you are unsure. “I am interested in…” is enough to begin.

Ask for honest feedback

What do they see as your strengths, and what would make the biggest difference?

Agree one or two actions

A project, some learning, a person to talk to. Small and definite is better than a long list.

Agree when you will talk again

Every quarter, twice a year, or another rhythm that suits you both.

Take this into the room. One simple screen with what you want and your questions, plus space for quick notes. Works on your phone.

If the conversation feels difficult
I am not sure what I want yet

That is a good place to start. Ask what your manager sees you doing well, and agree one small thing to try, such as a short project, to find out more.

I am happy in my current role

Growing in your role counts. Talk about how you would like it to stretch you, and what you would like to be known for.

I want a move my manager may not support

Ask what they would need to see from you to support it. Agree what that looks like and when you will review it together. CareerHub ↗ shows open roles across Holcim.

The feedback was hard to hear

It is fine to say you need time to think. Ask for a specific example, and arrange to pick it up again.
